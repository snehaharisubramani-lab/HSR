Our story

எங்கள் கதை

In a Tamil home, the day begins at the doorstep. Before anyone is awake to see it, a kolam is drawn in rice flour, small and careful, to welcome the day. Nobody asks for it. It is simply how a home shows love.

HSR Masala is made in that same spirit. Since 2000, in a Salem Chettiar kitchen, we have been roasting and grinding masalas the way they were taught to us, in small batches, with no preservatives and no artificial colours. We also bring back the old grains, kavuni, kollu, kambu and ragi, so a quick breakfast or soup tastes the way it used to.

If it would not go in our own kitchen, it does not go in your packet.
